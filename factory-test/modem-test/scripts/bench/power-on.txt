# Power-on timing and current: from VCC on to the first AT reply, IBUS every 50 ms.
# A provisioned EC2x boots at its stored rate: put "!baud 3000000" before !on.
!ibus 50
!off
sleep 3
!on
wait RDY 30
AT
wait OK 2
ATI
wait OK 2
AT+CGMM
wait OK 2
AT+CGMR
wait OK 2
sleep 5
AT+CFUN=4
wait OK 10
sleep 5
AT+QPOWD=1
wait POWERED DOWN 70
sleep 3
!off
sleep 3
!ibus 0
